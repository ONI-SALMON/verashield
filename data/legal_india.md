# Legal Framework for Deepfake Victims in India

## Key Laws That Protect You

- **IT Act 2008 – Section 66E**: Criminalizes capturing, publishing, or transmitting private images of a person without their consent. In practice, courts have extended this to cover morphed and AI-generated intimate images. Penalty: up to 3 years imprisonment and/or fine up to ₹2 lakh.

- **IT Act – Section 67B**: Specifically addresses child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in electronic form. If a deepfake involves a minor, this section carries a penalty of up to 7 years imprisonment and/or fine up to ₹10 lakh for first offence.

- **POCSO Act (Protection of Children from Sexual Offences)**: Provides enhanced protection when the victim is under 18. Any sexual content — including AI-generated deepfakes — involving a minor triggers POCSO, which carries stricter minimum sentences than general IT Act provisions. Cases are tried in special POCSO courts.

- **Digital Personal Data Protection Act (2023)**: Protects biometric data, including facial images. Unauthorized use of a person's likeness to generate synthetic media may constitute a breach under this Act.

## What You Can Do as a Victim

1. **File a cybercrime complaint online** at [cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in) — available 24/7, anonymous reporting supported.
2. **Call the National Cybercrime Helpline: 1930** — dedicated line for digital crime victims.
3. **Report to the platform** where the content appears — under IT Rules 2021, platforms must remove flagged content within 24 hours (72 hours for CSAM).
4. **Contact iCall TISS** (icallhelpline.org) for free psychosocial support.
5. **Contact Childline** at **1098** if you are under 18 — available 24/7, free, confidential.
